03

About

A product has to look like it deserves your card number — and load before you lose patience.

I like the unglamorous half of the job: the payment webhook that must never double-charge, the service key that must never reach the browser, the cron that has to fire at 9am whether or not anyone is watching.

Most of what I build targets the Algerian market — local payment rails, Arabic and French side by side, users who are rightly careful about paying an unknown site. That constraint made me obsessive about trust and speed.

I work end to end and I’m comfortable owning a product alone, from schema to deploy.

  1. 01Ship a small, real thing first — then grow it.
  2. 02Design tokens before components, every time.
  3. 03Security boundaries written down, not assumed.
  4. 04Plain language in every handoff.
